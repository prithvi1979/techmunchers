Advertiser FAQs

Questions from advertisers

Everything advertisers need to know about setting up campaigns, sourcing traffic, and optimizing performance.

How do I launch my first campaign?

Sign up on the platform, choose the campaign type, and work with our team to configure targeting, budget, and creatives. We support display, native, video, and mobile campaigns.

What traffic sources are available?

We offer our own premium publisher network plus external partners like Google Ads, Yahoo DSP, and other high-quality exchanges to maximize reach and performance.

How is campaign optimization handled?

Our optimization team continuously monitors performance, adjusts bids, tests creatives, and refines targeting to help you achieve the best possible ROI.

Publisher FAQs

Questions from publishers

Common publisher concerns around monetization, inventory quality, and payout reliability.

How do I join the publisher network?

Complete the publisher sign-up form and our onboarding team will review your site inventory and traffic quality. Once approved, you can start monetizing with premium campaigns.

What kinds of publishers perform best?

High-quality, brand-safe sites with engaged audiences perform best. We also favor publishers with clear content verticals and good viewability metrics.

When do publishers get paid?

Publishers receive payouts based on our payment terms, typically every 30 days after account verification. We offer secure payout options and transparent revenue reporting.

Affiliate FAQs

Questions from affiliates

Answers for affiliates about earning commissions, tracking referrals, and promotional support.

How much can I earn as an affiliate?

Affiliates can earn up to 30% commission on qualified referrals, with recurring revenue for active accounts and bonus incentives for high performance.

How do you track my referrals?

We use unique affiliate links and tracking codes to attribute leads and conversions accurately. Your affiliate dashboard shows referrals, commission status, and performance details.

What promotional support do affiliates receive?

Affiliates receive marketing materials, campaign-ready creatives, email templates, and performance guidance to help drive conversions and increase sales.
