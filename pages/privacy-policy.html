Privacy Policy

Your privacy at techmunchers.com

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when advertisers, publishers, affiliates, and visitors use our platform.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information from your use of our services, and technical data from your device. This helps us deliver relevant ad services, manage accounts, and support our advertising ecosystem.

  • ✓ Contact details and account registration data
  • ✓ Campaign and conversion performance data
  • ✓ Website usage and referral information
  • ✓ Device, browser, and IP address details

How We Use Your Data

We use personal information to operate our platform, improve services, and communicate with you. This includes enabling campaign management, publisher monetization, affiliate tracking, and legal compliance.

  • ✓ Deliver ad services and campaign optimization
  • ✓ Manage accounts, billing, and support
  • ✓ Prevent fraud and protect the platform
  • ✓ Share performance insights with advertisers and publishers

Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information with trusted partners, service providers, and regulatory authorities when required. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

  • ✓ Service providers that support our platform
  • ✓ Advertising partners for campaign delivery
  • ✓ Legal authorities when legally required
  • ✓ Company affiliates and third-party network partners

Security and Contact

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect data. If you have questions about this policy or your information, email us at legal@techmunchers.com.
